Questions

Questions people ask.

Book a walk-round
How much does it cost?

Every premises is different, so we price from your walk-round. You get a written quote within 24 hours, with a set price for once, twice or three times a week. Prices exclude VAT: we're not VAT registered.

When can you start?

Our first cleans start in January 2028. Book a walk-round now. Businesses on two or more visits a week can claim one of the five Founding Five places.

What if I'm not happy with the first clean?

Then you don't pay for it.

Can I choose when you clean?

Yes. You choose how often (once, twice or three times a week) and when it happens.

Is there a minimum visit?

Yes, 2 hours a visit. That gives every clean time to work through your checklist properly.

Will it be the same cleaner every time?

We can't promise that, but whoever is on the job works to the same checklist and the same standard, and their work is checked.

Are you insured?

Our public and employers' liability cover will be in place before our first clean, and we'll show you the certificate.

How do you get in?

Tell us how the keys or access work. Access codes and key details are kept private and only shared with the cleaner on the job, on the day.

Do I need to supply anything?

No. We bring all products, equipment and bin bags, and restock your toilet roll, soap and paper towels.

Why aren't you the cheapest?

Because every clean is done to a written checklist, photographed, and spot-checked with a scored audit. Cheap cleaning usually means rushed visits and a standard that changes week to week.

Do you do end of tenancy or home cleans?

Not yet. Property cleaning is coming soon. For now we clean businesses only.
