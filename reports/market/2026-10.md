## The short answer

Will the range sell? Partly. Four of the seven sit in categories where other Etsy sellers show real sales: brag book/career trackers, 5 Whys, RCA and maintenance dashboards. Three (MTBA Assist Tracker, KPI Impact Calculator and, less so, the Breakdown Dashboard) have little or no sign of buyers searching Etsy for them. Nothing in this report proves Kaizen XP will sell. It shows where buyers already spend money, and at what price.

## How this was done, and what is missing

- **Etsy blocked direct access.** Every WebFetch to etsy.com failed (DNS: "getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND www.etsy.com"). So did gumroad.com, makerwords.com and learnleansigma.com. Reddit is blocked to the search tool ("domains are not accessible to our user agent").
- **What I used instead:** web search restricted to etsy.com, which returns listing titles and summary snippets with prices, review counts and sales counts. Every number below comes from those snippets, with the listing URL.
- **Read these numbers with care:**
  - Etsy shows the shop's total sales on a listing page, not the listing's. Where a snippet gives "X sales", treat it as shop-level unless stated otherwise.
  - Snippets are not dated. Prices may have changed. Most prices are in US dollars.
  - Where I compare dollars with pounds I use about $1.30 to £1. That rate is my assumption, not a quoted rate. Check it on the day.
- **Missing data (please supply if you can):**
  - No eRank or other keyword export in data/exports/. So I have **no search volume numbers** for any term on Etsy or Google. Demand below is shown by what sellers sell and buyers review, not by search counts. A free eRank account export for the 15 terms listed under each product would fill this gap.
  - reports/market/buyer-requests.md does not exist yet (no buyer messages).
  - reports/weekly/ is empty and data/exports/stats.csv has only a header row. Nothing is live, so there is no own-shop sales data.
- **One sign of searching:** Etsy has auto-generated "market" pages for many of these terms (for example etsy.com/market/root_cause_analysis_template, /downtime_tracker, /kaizen_template, /brag_book_template). My understanding is that Etsy builds these pages around terms people search for. That is my inference, not an Etsy statement, and it does not say how many people search.

**Verdict: Weak on Etsy.**

**Somewhere else?** Probably better shown to people who already know the term: maintenance and CI groups on LinkedIn, linking to Gumroad or Payhip. That is a suggestion; I could not check LinkedIn or Reddit.
