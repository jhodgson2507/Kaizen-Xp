## 2. Worked examples and totals contain wrong numbers a buyer can check (CI-002)

- **What is wrong:** five places in the range show a wrong count, rank, definition or total, in a shop whose whole edge is "someone who knows CI".
- **Evidence (all checked by me):**
  - CI Starter Kit page 5, "WHY IT WORKS": "Three more whys found a fix". There are four more (whys 2 to 5).
  - MTBA Assist Tracker page 5 table: "Reset or restart 14 / 28 / rank 3" above "Re-handle or re-work 6 / 30 / rank 4". The product's own rule is rank by minutes lost.
  - KPI Impact Calculator, Improvement Log: the invented example row 9 (£4,290, 234 hours) sits inside the running totals (E5 `=COUNTA(C9:C58)`, F5 `=SUM(F9:F58)`). Recalculated in LibreOffice, a brand new file shows "1 improvement, £4,290 a year saved, all fixes". A buyer who adds their own rows below instead of overwriting row 9 carries an invented £4,290 into the number they show their manager. Neither QA nor UX report caught this.
  - KPI Calculator B44: `=IF(N(C13)=0,"Fill in the cream boxes...","I reduced ... from "&C13&" to "...` so a waste-only problem never gets its sentence, and BEFORE only says "from 3 to 3 times". The worked example reads "from 4 to 1 times a shift".

- **Impact:** High. A wrong number in the worked example is the fastest way to a "doesn't know what he's talking about" review. The £4,290 carry-over could put an invented saving in a buyer's appraisal.
- **Root cause:** product-builder.md says "render every page and look at it yourself" but has no step to recompute worked examples and totals. QA is the only place the sums get done, so errors survive until the end of the line.
- **Countermeasure:** product-builder: fix all five in one rebuild (CI build.py "Four more whys"; MTBA build.py swap the two rows; KPI log totals start at row 10 or the example moves to the Example tab; KPI B44 branches on E36; Breakdown C14 wording to match C59).
