# Kaizen XP: shop rules for every agent

## What this is
Kaizen XP is Josh's Etsy shop selling fillable, printable continuous
improvement and career tools to frontline shift workers. Josh is a process
technician and stand-in shift manager with hands-on TPM, RCFA, 5 Whys, Kaizen,
OEE and MTBA experience. That real shop floor experience is the shop's edge.

## Who buys
Operators, technicians, team leaders and supervisors on shifts, in
manufacturing, warehouses, retail and food. They want to get better at the
job and get noticed for it. Many open files on a phone during a break.

## Brand
- Name: Kaizen XP. Black #0d0d0d and gold #c9a84c, cream #f3efe4 panels.
- Helvetica in PDFs, Arial in Excel.
- Motto on every product: "Do what nobody else can be bothered to do, and
  you'll get what nobody else gets."
- Every product: fillable on screen, printable on paper, a worked example,
  a back page linking the rest of the range, and the line:
  "© 2026 Kaizen XP. Licensed for one person's use. Please do not share or resell."

## Voice
Plain, direct, frontline. Written by someone who has worked the shift.
No corporate jargon, no hype, no emoji. NO EM DASHES anywhere: use hyphens,
colons or a new sentence.

## How we work
- Agents draft. Josh publishes. Nothing goes live without him.
- One change per listing per fortnight, logged in decisions.md with the
  number it should move.
- Every new or changed product passes product-qa and usability-checker
  before it is listed.
- The ci-challenger reviews everything weekly. Open issues live in issues.md.

## Never
- Fake, bought or swapped reviews, or asking buyers to change a review.
- Fake sales (raising a price to make a discount look bigger).
- Copying another seller's wording, layout or product.
- Real employer names, data, documents or photos in any product. Worked
  examples are invented.
- Income or promotion guarantees.
- Saving buyers' personal details outside Etsy.
- Spending money (ads, tools) without Josh setting a limit first.
